Running can be for me.

To make running feel accessible, I defined strict strategic roles: Nike acts as the motivator, the product is the helper, and the human owns the emotional payoff—simply feeling alive. This meant moving away from high-gloss studio aesthetics.

As the Art Director, I established a raw, cinematic visual world. We paired authentic street photography with bold, unpolished typography to create the 'Run For Life' identity. By grounding the campaign in the real, imperfect movement of the city, we created a premium editorial look that gave ordinary people permission to claim running as their own.

01 / OOH

Make the city notice. The OOH system evolves from human emotion to mapping the city, proving that running belongs in everyday life.

02 / Social

Make the campaign personal. The feed drops the distance, using intimate, feed-native photography to bridge the gap between observing and participating.

03 / Experiential

Don't tell people why they run. Let them feel it. A travelling activation where the experience matters far more than the performance.

04 / Digital

Your run. Your pace. Your journey. The digital touchpoint doesn't tell you how to run—it remembers why you started.

05 / Retail

From the street to the store. Retail continues the conversation: Where are you now? What's next?
