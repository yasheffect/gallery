Beats, Rhymes & Life: The Travels of A Tribe Called Quest is a documentary directed by Michael Rapaport that chronicles the rise, impact, and complex relationships of one of hip-hop’s most influential groups. Having released five gold and platinum albums in just eight years, A Tribe Called Quest’s sudden breakup in 1998 shocked fans and the industry alike. In 2008, Rapaport joined Q-Tip, Phife Dawg, Ali Shaheed Muhammad, and Jarobi White on their reunion tour, capturing the creative magic, personal tensions, and backstage conflicts that continue to shape their legacy. Through rare archival footage, behind the scenes moments, and interviews with hip-hop luminaries such as the Beastie Boys, Kanye West, Pharrell, Mos Def, and Busta Rhymes, the film offers an intimate portrait of the group’s artistry, influence, and the possibility of reconciliation.
